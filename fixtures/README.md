# Synthetic fixtures

Everything here is invented for the demo. "Fleetwing Express" is a fictional, FedEx-inspired
delivery company. The workers, their CVs, employers, LinkedIn handles and emails (`example.net`,
an IANA reserved domain) do not refer to real people or companies. The training policy is an
example policy written for this demo, not a real company's policy.

- `workers/rosa-experienced/` — six years of parcel delivery, CDL-B, used scanners and box trucks.
- `workers/theo-beginner/` — retail and food-service background, no parcel delivery. Their CV
  contains an embedded prompt-injection line to show that CV text is treated as data only, and
  their questionnaire claims bike-courier experience the CV does not mention (a conflict that
  must go to manager review).
